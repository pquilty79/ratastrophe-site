Ratastrophe / Privacy policy

Your colony.
Your device.

Effective and last updated: November 9, 2026.

This policy describes the current Ratastrophe app for iPhone and iPad and its support website. Contact customer service using the email address below.

The short version

No app account is required. Your campaign and achievements stay on your device. The free app includes Google AdMob banner and occasional full-screen ads, configured for non-personalized advertising. Google may process technical information for ads and consent. An optional US$0.99 one-time purchase removes both ad formats. Apple handles purchases. Customer service receives the information you choose to send by email.

Game information stored on your device

The app saves the active campaign, including colonies and their traits, quantities, cash and supplies, progress, story choices, queued events, and unread reports. It also saves discovered achievements and settings, including the locally recorded Remove Ads entitlement. These records let you resume the game and retain discoveries between campaigns.

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or photo-library access. Your saved campaign is not uploaded to a developer-operated server. In-game followers, businesses, and online contacts are fictional game mechanics, not real social-media or dark-web activity. Actual advertisements are handled separately by Google AdMob as described below.

Local storage, retention, and backups

Saved data remains in the app’s local storage until it is replaced, reset, or removed. Starting New Game replaces the active campaign after confirmation when a save exists. Achievements and the verified purchase entitlement are retained separately. There is no developer-hosted cloud save or app account.

App data may be included in iCloud or computer device backups according to your settings. Apple or your chosen backup system handles those copies; the developer cannot access them. Removing active app data does not automatically remove older backups or copies on other devices.

App Store purchases

Ratastrophe is free to download. Its optional Remove Ads purchase is a one-time, non-consumable purchase priced at US$0.99 in the US storefront; Apple displays local pricing and applicable taxes. It removes both banner and full-screen ads, with no subscription. The purchase uses Apple’s StoreKit framework. Apple processes the payment and manages the transaction under its own terms and privacy practices. The app checks the product and verified entitlement to provide the purchased feature and restore it when requested. Customer service does not receive your payment card details through the app, and we do not operate a purchase-account server.

Purchase records held by Apple are separate from local game data. See Apple’s privacy policy. Use the same Apple Account and Options → Restore Purchases to restore an eligible purchase.

Advertising, consent, and diagnostics

Ad-supported versions use the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform. These services can process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, and crash or performance information. Google uses information for ad delivery, measurement, security, fraud prevention, and related analytics, and may share it with advertising partners as explained in its policies.

The app requests non-personalized ads, disables Google’s publisher first-party ID feature and automatic collection through location APIs, and does not request Apple’s cross-app tracking permission. Non-personalized advertising can still process the technical information described above. It does not mean that no information reaches Google.

Google’s consent platform checks which messages apply and gathers choices where needed. Ads are requested only when that platform allows them. Where required, Options → Ad Privacy Choices lets you revisit your decision. Declining consent or opting out can change which ads are available; it does not prevent you from playing or require an ad-removal purchase.

Once Remove Ads is verified, the app removes both placements and does not request additional banner or full-screen ads. This does not erase information Google has already received. Google controls retention and deletion for information it processes under its policies. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information for advertising.

Ratastrophe does not add a separate gameplay analytics service or upload saved colonies to a developer-operated telemetry system. We do not sell or rent personal information. Apple may separately process App Store, transaction, device, crash, or diagnostic information under its privacy practices and the sharing settings you choose.

Earlier playtest versions displayed only fictional, locally generated sponsor messages. These Google advertising disclosures apply to versions with the AdMob integration.

When you contact support

If you email supportchesstimewars@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other details you choose to include. We use this information to respond, investigate app issues, consider feedback, and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card information, or unrelated personal details.

Support email is handled by our email provider, Google, under its privacy policy. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request, resolve the issue, and meet applicable obligations. You may request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address with “Ratastrophe privacy request” in the subject. We cannot retrieve or delete local game data from your device remotely.

This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. The site does not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, embedded social widgets, or a contact form. Its images and styles are served with the site. Email links open your chosen email service; nothing is submitted by merely opening a page.

GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser information, and requested pages, to deliver and protect its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. External services linked from the site operate under their own policies.

Your choices and deletion

  • Replace the active campaign: choose New Game from the main menu and confirm. This starts a fresh campaign; achievements and purchase entitlement remain.
  • Remove all local app data: use Delete App in your device’s settings, rather than Offload App. This removes the local app container, including the campaign, achievements, and preferences. Back up anything you want to retain first.
  • Manage backups and other devices: remove unwanted copies separately through the relevant iCloud, computer, or device settings.
  • Manage purchases: deleting the app does not delete Apple’s transaction records or cancel ownership of a non-consumable purchase. Apple handles purchase history and refund requests.
  • Manage ads and consent: use Ad Privacy Choices in Options when available. The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase disables both banner and full-screen ads. Google’s policies explain choices and requests relating to information Google has already received.
  • Control what you send: support email is optional. Send only the details you want us to receive, and request deletion of correspondence through the support address.

Ratastrophe has no developer-hosted account to delete. Advertising consent choices, local game data, Apple purchase records, and support correspondence are separate; changing or deleting one does not automatically delete the others.

Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to customer service at supportchesstimewars@gmail.com. For help with the game, visit Ratastrophe Support.
