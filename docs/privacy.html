Ratastrophe / Privacy policy

Your colony.
Your device.

Effective and last updated: November 9, 2026.

This policy describes the current Ratastrophe app for iPhone and iPad and its support website. The developer is Patrick Quilty.

The short version

No app account is required. Your campaign, achievements, and preferences are stored on your device. Ratastrophe does not upload them to a developer-operated server, include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, or track you across apps. Apple handles App Store purchases. We receive the information you choose to send when you email support.

Game information stored on your device

The app saves the active campaign, including colonies and their traits, quantities, cash and supplies, progress, story choices, queued events, and unread reports. It also saves discovered achievements and settings, including the locally recorded Remove Ads entitlement. These records let you resume the game and retain discoveries between campaigns.

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photo-library access. Gameplay information is not automatically sent to the developer. In-game followers, advertising, businesses, and online contacts are fictional game mechanics, not real social-media or dark-web activity.

Local storage, retention, and backups

Saved data remains in the app’s local storage until it is replaced, reset, or removed. Starting New Game replaces the active campaign after confirmation when a save exists. Achievements and the verified purchase entitlement are retained separately. There is no developer-hosted cloud save or app account.

App data may be included in iCloud or computer device backups according to your settings. Apple or your chosen backup system handles those copies; the developer cannot access them. Removing active app data does not automatically remove older backups or copies on other devices.

App Store purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase uses Apple’s StoreKit framework. Apple processes the payment and manages the transaction under its own terms and privacy practices. The app checks the product and verified entitlement to provide the purchased feature and restore it when requested. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the app and does not operate a purchase-account server.

Purchase records held by Apple are separate from local game data. See Apple’s privacy policy. Use the same Apple Account and Options → Restore Purchases to restore an eligible purchase.

Advertising, tracking, and diagnostics

The current app displays locally generated fictional sponsor messages as part of the game. It does not connect to a third-party advertising network, use an advertising identifier, run third-party analytics, or transmit gameplay telemetry to a developer-operated service. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Apple may separately process App Store, transaction, device, crash, or diagnostic information under its own privacy practices and the sharing settings you choose. That is separate from an automatic upload of your saved campaign by Ratastrophe.

When you contact support

If you email supportchesstimewars@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other details you choose to include. We use this information to respond, investigate app issues, consider feedback, and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card information, or unrelated personal details.

Support email is handled by our email provider, Google, under its privacy policy. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request, resolve the issue, and meet applicable obligations. You may request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address with “Ratastrophe privacy request” in the subject. We cannot retrieve or delete local game data from your device remotely.

This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. The site does not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, embedded social widgets, or a contact form. Its images and styles are served with the site. Email links open your chosen email service; nothing is submitted by merely opening a page.

GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser information, and requested pages, to deliver and protect its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. External services linked from the site operate under their own policies.

Your choices and deletion

  • Replace the active campaign: choose New Game from the main menu and confirm. This starts a fresh campaign; achievements and purchase entitlement remain.
  • Remove all local app data: use Delete App in your device’s settings, rather than Offload App. This removes the local app container, including the campaign, achievements, and preferences. Back up anything you want to retain first.
  • Manage backups and other devices: remove unwanted copies separately through the relevant iCloud, computer, or device settings.
  • Manage purchases: deleting the app does not delete Apple’s transaction records or cancel ownership of a non-consumable purchase. Apple handles purchase history and refund requests.
  • Control what you send: support email is optional. Send only the details you want us to receive, and request deletion of correspondence through the support address.

Ratastrophe has no developer-hosted account to delete. There is no app tracking consent to revoke in the current version because the app does not perform cross-app tracking.

Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to Patrick Quilty at supportchesstimewars@gmail.com. For help with the game, visit Ratastrophe Support.
