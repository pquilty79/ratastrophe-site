Your very reasonable business plan

One cage today.
Who knows tomorrow?

You start as a rat enthusiast with a little cash and four pets. What happens next depends on the colonies you raise and the choices you make.

01 /

Build the operation.

Buy cages, make room for new colonies, and breed for the traits your buyers want. Grow from an apartment to increasingly ambitious surroundings.

02 /

Encourage their talents.

Start with tricks and puzzles. Research strange genes, train specialized crews, and send your rats out to do something useful. “Useful” becomes a flexible word.

03 /

Answer the door.

A visitor, a sponsor, a promising offer. Each turn can bring a decision with lasting consequences. The full terms tend to reveal themselves later.

Small choices. Large rodents.

A beloved pet business.
Or a change of management.

Become a successful breeder who gets the world to love pet rats. Or keep experimenting until your smartest employee has opinions about who should be in charge.

Branching stories, discoveries, and endings give you reasons to try a different path. Achievements on the main menu offer a glimpse of the possibilities.
